# Tablo Support

Tablo helps you turn your book collection into a beautiful virtual coffee table. Scan a book barcode or enter its ISBN, arrange covers, choose a surface, add decorative stickers, and share your finished Tablo.

## Contact

For help, email [info@adeleroberts.com](mailto:info@adeleroberts.com).

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful, but please avoid sending private or sensitive information.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a book?

Open the Library and tap **Add**. Enter the ISBN printed on the book, or choose **Scan Barcode** and point the camera at its barcode.

### Why is a cover, title, or author missing?

Tablo retrieves book information from Open Library. Some editions have incomplete metadata or no cover image. You can tap a book in the Library to edit its title, author, and cover.

### Where is my library stored?

Your books and Tablo arrangements are stored locally on your device. Tablo doesn't require an account and doesn't currently provide cloud synchronisation.

### Why can't I scan a barcode?

Barcode scanning requires a supported iPhone or iPad, camera permission, and an available camera. You can always enter the ISBN manually.

### How do I remove a book?

Use the remove control on a book card to delete it from your Library. When editing your table, select a placed book and use its remove control to take that copy off the table.
